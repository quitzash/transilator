How to use this site

  1. To speak with the microphone, use Chrome or Microsoft Edge, open the site through start-server.bat (localhost:8000), and allow the microphone when asked. You need internet.
  2. Tap the big button saying "Tap here and speak", then say a sentence slowly and clearly.
  3. The translation is written in large letters and said aloud in the other person’s language.
  4. If the spoken voice is too fast, turn on "Turtle speech".
  5. Prefer typing? Use the text box and press "Translate and speak".
  6. Stuck on an idea? Under "Common phrases", tap one such as "I need a doctor."
  7. Use the A / A / A+ buttons to change text size, and the "Dark" button for dark colors.
  8. The "Two people" mode lets two people send and hear messages in their own languages.
Tip: every translation is saved under "Recent translations". Use the sound button beside one to hear it again.
